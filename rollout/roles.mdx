---
title: "Who does what each day"
description: "A day with Operant Health for the front desk, medical assistants, providers and the office manager."
---

Use this page to walk each person through their day before go-live.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Front desk">
    **App role:** Medical Assistant

    **Before clinic**
    - Get the day's visits in: add them, scan the printed schedule, or import from Practice Fusion. See [your schedule](/visits/schedule).
    - Send intake links to patients who haven't done intake. See [patient intake](/visits/patient-intake).

    **At check-in**
    - If intake isn't **Complete**, tap **Open intake form** and hand the patient the clinic iPad.

    **Through the day**
    - Upload outside records to **Documents** and attach them. See [document inbox](/practice/document-inbox).
    - Work the follow-ups in **Tasks**. See [tasks](/practice/tasks).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Medical assistant">
    **App role:** Medical Assistant

    - Check that each patient's intake is **Complete** before rooming.
    - Confirm the chief complaint on the visit.
    - Make sure the recording device for the room is charged and signed in.
    - After the visit, help with the forms on the visit's **Forms** tab. See [forms and patient guides](/notes/forms-and-guides).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Provider">
    **App role:** Physician or Physician Assistant

    **Before each visit**
    - Read the **Pre-visit briefing** on the visit's **Overview**.

    **During the visit**
    - Start recording from **Home**, then see the patient as usual. See [record a visit](/visits/record-a-visit).

    **After the visit**
    - Review, edit and sign the note. See [review and sign notes](/notes/review-and-sign).
    - Add a provider note if there's anything you want on the record in your own words.
    - Check the patient guide and send it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Office manager">
    **App role:** Administrator

    - Lead the rollout. See the [rollout plan](/rollout/overview).
    - Invite and remove staff. See [invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team).
    - Keep visit types, templates, intake questions and forms up to date with your providers.
    - At the end of each day, check that every visit has a note, and that nothing in **Recordings** shows **Processing failed**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

There's no separate front desk role. Invite front desk staff as **Medical Assistant**, or as **Administrator** if they also manage settings.
