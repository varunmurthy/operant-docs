---
title: "Invite your team"
description: "Add providers and staff, and choose what each person can do."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users">
    Go to **Settings** → **Users** and click **Add user**, then **Invite a user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Enter their **Name** and **Email**, choose a **Role**, and click **Send invitation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They create a password">
    They open the email, enter a **Password**, confirm it, and click **Create password**. Then they can sign in on the web and in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a role

| Role | Who it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Physician** | Providers who see patients and sign notes. |
| **Physician Assistant** | PAs who see patients and sign notes. |
| **Medical Assistant** | MAs and front desk staff. |
| **Administrator** | The office manager. Administrators also manage visit types and report templates for the whole practice. |

Only a **Physician** or **Physician Assistant** can be the treating provider on a visit.

## Manage someone's access

In **Settings** → **Users**, use the actions on each person's row:

- **Resend invite** if their invitation expired or got lost.
- **Edit** to change their name or role.
- **Deactivate** when someone leaves. It takes effect immediately.
- **Reactivate** to restore access.
