---
title: "Document inbox"
description: "Upload outside records and file them to the right patient."
---

Use **Documents** for anything that arrives from outside: referrals, imaging reports, records from another practice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Click **Upload files**. You can add PDFs, images, or a ZIP of several files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Each upload is read and matched to a patient. Filter by **Needs review** and click **Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach">
    In **Review and attach**, click **Attach**, or **Attach all** for a batch. If the patient isn't in your practice yet, click **New patient**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a document couldn't be read, it shows under **Failed**. Click **Retry scan**.

Attached documents feed the pre-visit briefing for that patient's next visit.
