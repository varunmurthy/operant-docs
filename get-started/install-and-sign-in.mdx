---
title: "Install the app and sign in"
description: "Get Operant Health on your iPhone or iPad, and sign in on the web."
---

You use the same email and password everywhere.

## On iPhone or iPad

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Operant Health">
    Use the install link your Operant Health contact sends you. The app appears on your home screen as **Operant Health**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then tap **Sign in**. iCloud Keychain can fill in your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow the microphone">
    The first time you record, the app asks for microphone access. Tap **Allow**. Without it, the app can't record visits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## On the web

Go to [operanthealth.com/sign-in](https://operanthealth.com/sign-in) and sign in with the same email and password. Use Chrome if your practice will send notes to Practice Fusion. See [send notes to your EHR](/notes/send-to-your-ehr).

Operant Health is moving from orthoshealth.ai to operanthealth.com. Old orthoshealth.ai links and bookmarks still work during the move, but update your bookmarks to operanthealth.com.

## Forgot your password?

Tap or click **Forgot?**, enter your email and choose **Send reset link**. Open the link from your email and set a new password. If you're on the app, go back to it and sign in.
