---
title: "Patient intake"
description: "Get intake done before the provider walks in."
---

Every patient visit has an intake panel. It shows **Not started**, **In progress** or **Complete**, and the patient's answers appear there once they finish.

## Send or hand over the intake form

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone and iPad">
    Open the visit from **Schedule**, then choose:

    | Option | When to use it |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Open intake form** | The patient is in the office. Opens the form on this device so you can hand them the clinic iPad. |
    | The QR code | The patient uses their own phone. Tap the code to enlarge it, and they scan it. |
    | **Send email** | The patient is at home. You'll see **Secure email sent**. |
    | **Copy link** | To send the link another way. You'll see **Link copied**. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    Open the visit and go to the **Intake** tab. Click **Send email**, or **Copy link** to share it another way.

    From the schedule's side panel you can also **Generate link**, **Open link**, **Copy link** or make a **New link**. The panel shows how long the current link stays active.

    The QR code is only in the iPhone and iPad app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Sending by email needs an email address on the patient's record and your practice's schedule time zone set.

## How long links last

An intake link works for 24 hours after it's created. Once the patient opens it, they have 3 hours to finish.

If a link has expired, the patient sees a **Send me a new link** button. It emails a fresh link to the address on file.

## What the patient sees

A short check-in that takes about 3 minutes. They confirm their details, answer your questions (by typing, or by tapping **Tap to speak**), review their answers and tap **Send to care team**.

If your practice checks insurance, they also see **Verify your ID and insurance**. It walks them through one step at a time:

1. **Photo ID**. If an ID is on file from a previous visit, they see it and tap **Yes, this is correct** or **Take a new photo**.
2. **Primary insurance**. If a card is on file, they see it and tap **Yes, this is correct** or **Something changed**. They can also tap **I no longer have insurance (self-pay)**.
3. **Secondary insurance**. They confirm the plan on file, or answer **Do you have a second insurance plan?** This step is skipped if they chose self-pay.

Each step collapses to a one-line summary once it's answered. When nothing is on file, they take one photo of their ID and a photo of each side of their insurance card.

They can tap **Skip and complete it in the office** to finish this part with your front desk instead.

The **Request insurance information** link you send from a patient's chart works the same way.

## Change the questions

Go to **Settings** → **Intake Forms**. Click **Add question** to add one, then **Save**.

## Paper or outside forms

An uploaded paper form can stand in for intake. The visit shows **Outside intake**. Tap **Open source form** to see it.
