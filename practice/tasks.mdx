title: "Tasks"
description: "Follow-up work for the front desk and office staff, in one queue."
---

**Tasks** collects the follow-up work that comes out of visits, so nothing depends on someone remembering it.

Open **More** → **Tasks** in the app.

## Work the queue

1. Start in **To do**.
2. Open a task. Use **Review visit forms** to see the forms attached to that visit.
3. Tap **Update status** when you've done the work. Add **Work notes** so the next person knows what happened. A task can't be completed without them.
4. Finished and cancelled tasks move to **Completed / cancelled**.
