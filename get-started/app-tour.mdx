title: "Find your way around the app"
description: "What each tab in the OrthOS app is for, on iPhone and iPad."
---

The iPhone and iPad app is called **OrthOS**. iPad shows a wider layout with fewer tabs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    | Tab | What it's for |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Cases** | Your patients' cases. |
    | **Schedule** | Today's visits, surgeries and events. Switch between **Schedule**, **Day** and **Week**. |
    | **Home** | Today at a glance. Start a recording from a visit card. |
    | **Reports** | Notes waiting for review, and signed notes. |
    | **More** | Patients, Recordings, Templates, Tasks, Settings and **Sign out**. |

    iPhone stays in portrait.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPad">
    | Tab | What it's for |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Home** | Today's visits, recording, and the post-visit checklist. |
    | **Schedule** | Visits by day or week. |
    | **Reports** | Notes waiting for review, and signed notes. |
    | **More** | Cases, Patients, Recordings, Templates, Tasks, Settings and **Sign out**. |

    iPad works in any orientation. In narrow Split View or Slide Over, the app switches to the iPhone layout.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A red number on **Reports** counts new notes that are ready for you.

## Check your app version

Open **More**. The version and build number are at the bottom of the screen. Support may ask for them.
