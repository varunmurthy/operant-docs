## Forms

Each visit has a **Forms** tab with its **Visit documents**. Click **Add** to attach a form from your library.

When you sign a note, forms linked to that note are filled in from it.

Your practice starts with these forms:

- Authorization Request
- Cardiac Clearance
- Ambulatory Surgery Scheduling Form
- Hospital Scheduling Form
- Hospital Physician Pre-Admission Orders
- Home Health Referral
- Referral Request
- CPM Order + PT Protocol
- Implant / Equipment Request
- Anesthesia Clearance Packet

### Add your own forms

Go to **Settings** → **Document Library**, then click **New Document** or **Open designer**. Click **Save draft** while you work, and **Publish** when it's ready to use. The library is in beta.

Archiving a form removes it from the library. Copies already on visits stay.

## Patient guides

After each visit, a patient guide is drafted from the note: what you discussed and the patient's next steps, in plain language.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the guide">
    Open the note and click **Patient guide**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review it">
    Edit anything you like. Click **Preview** to see what the patient sees, or **Regenerate** for a fresh draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save** publishes the guide to the patient's private page. Nothing is sent yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Send to patient**, then **Send by email** or **Open text message**. The patient needs an email address or mobile number on file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a patient replies with a question, click **Respond**. To take a guide down, click **Revoke link**.

### Set your practice's guide design

Go to **Settings** → **Patient Guide** and click **Create Patient Guide**. Click **Save as current** to use it for new guides.
