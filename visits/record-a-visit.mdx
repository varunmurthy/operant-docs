---
title: "Record a visit"
description: "Capture the conversation so your note is drafted for you."
---

Start from **Home** in the app, or from the visit on the web. The audio is saved on your device first, so a dropped connection doesn't lose the visit.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find the visit">
        Open **Home**. Each visit on today's schedule has its own card.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start recording">
        Tap **Start recording** on the visit card. Recording starts right away and is linked to that visit.

        To record without a scheduled visit, tap **+** and choose **New recording**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pause or stop">
        Use the pause and stop buttons on the recording screen. If you move to another screen, a bar at the bottom shows **Recording** or **Paused** with **Finish**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPad">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start recording">
        On **Home**, tap **Record Patient Visit**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pause or finish">
        Use pause and play while you talk. Tap **Finish** when the visit ends.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Only one recording can run at a time. If you start a second one, the app tells you a recording is already active.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    Open the visit and click **Start recording**. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.

    The audio is saved in your browser while you record. Stay in the same browser until it has uploaded.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After you finish

The app opens the post-visit checklist. It tracks four things for the visit:

1. **The note.** Tap **Review & sign** when it's ready.
2. **Your own note.** Tap **Add note** to dictate or type anything you want on the record.
3. **Forms.** Tap **Open forms**.
4. **The patient guide.** Tap **View**.

While the note is drafted, the **In flight** section on the record screen shows its progress: uploading, transcribing, then **Report ready**.

<Tip>
  Recording keeps your iPhone or iPad screen awake, so it won't lock mid-visit.
</Tip>

If nobody has spoken for 5 minutes, you'll be asked whether you're still recording. If no one answers within a minute, recording pauses.

## Dictate a note of your own

A **provider note** is separate from the visit recording. Use it to dictate your own findings or plan.

- On iPad, tap **Provider Note** on **Home**.
- After a visit, tap **Add note** on the post-visit checklist.
