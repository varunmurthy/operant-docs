This plan takes about three weeks. Your office manager leads the rollout, and your Operant Health contact sets things up alongside you.

## Before you start

Pick these first. Everything else follows from them.

- **A practice lead.** Usually the office manager. They own the plan and the go-live date.
- **A pilot provider.** One provider uses it first, for a week, before everyone else.
- **A go-live date.** Avoid your busiest clinic days.
- **Devices.** Which iPhones or iPads will record in each exam room, and who carries them.

## Week 1: set up

| Task | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Create the practice account and invite your team | Practice lead |
| Add your visit types | Practice lead |
| Send us your note templates, macros and forms | Practice lead, providers |
| Install the app on each recording device and sign in | Each user |
| Walk through a test visit end to end | Pilot provider, practice lead |

## Week 2: pilot

- The pilot provider records their own visits for a full week.
- The front desk sends intake links for that provider's patients.
- Meet for 15 minutes at the end of each day. Note what to change in templates, forms and guides.

## Week 3: go live

- Every provider starts recording.
- Keep the 15-minute end-of-day check-in for the first week.
- Use the [go-live checklist](/rollout/go-live-checklist) on the first day.

## After 30 days

Review with your Operant Health contact:

- How many visits were recorded and signed.
- Where notes still need the most editing.
- What the front desk still does by hand.
