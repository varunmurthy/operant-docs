---
title: "Go-live checklist"
description: "Check these before the first patient on go-live day."
---

## The day before

- [ ] Every provider can sign in on their recording device.
- [ ] Microphone access is on for the app on every device. See [common issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues).
- [ ] Tomorrow's schedule is in the app, with patient names on each visit.
- [ ] The front desk knows how to send an intake link and open it on the clinic iPad.
- [ ] Each provider has reviewed and signed one test note.

## Go-live morning

- [ ] Devices are charged.
- [ ] Each provider knows where to tap to start recording. See [record a visit](/visits/record-a-visit).
- [ ] Someone is named to answer staff questions during clinic.

## End of the day

- [ ] Every visit has a note in **Reports**, or a recording that's still processing.
- [ ] Nothing shows **Processing failed** in **More** → **Recordings**. If it does, tap **Retry**.
- [ ] Unsigned notes are either signed or assigned to a provider for tomorrow.
- [ ] Write down what slowed anyone down, for the end-of-day check-in.
