---
title: "Common issues"
description: "Quick fixes for recording, notes, intake and sign-in problems."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Recording failed: microphone permission denied">
    The app can't use the microphone.

    1. Open the iPhone or iPad **Settings** app.
    2. Scroll to **OrthOS** and turn on **Microphone**.
    3. Go back to the app and start the recording again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Camera access needed or Photo access needed">
    Scanning a printed schedule uses the camera or your photos. In **Settings** → **OrthOS**, turn on **Camera** or **Photos**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A recording is stuck uploading">
    Check your connection. Uploads retry on their own and resume when you reopen the app.

    To check one recording, open **More** → **Recordings** and filter by **Processing**. Open it and tap **Retry**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Processing took too long">
    Open the recording from **More** → **Recordings** and tap **Retry**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recording needs recovery">
    The app kept your audio on the device but couldn't finish saving it. **Don't delete the app.** Contact support with your app version from the bottom of the **More** tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No report generated">
    Open the report and tap **Generate report** or **Retry report**. The audio is kept, so nothing needs to be re-recorded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't use AI editing on a note">
    Signed notes are locked. Tap **Reopen** to make changes. AI dictation is also paused while a visit recording is running.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unsaved changes">
    Notes don't save on their own. Tap **Save** before you leave, or choose **Keep editing** when the app asks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The note is still being generated">
    The page shows **Report is being generated**. Click **Refresh now** to check again.

    If it's still not ready after 5 minutes, open the recording and click **Retry report**, or open the note and click **Generate report**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Saved locally — retrying upload (web)">
    The recording is saved in your browser and the upload will retry. Stay in the same browser, and don't clear its history or site data until the upload finishes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Saved elsewhere">
    Someone saved this note on another device while you were editing. Click **Reload latest** to see their version, or **Copy my text** first so you don't lose yours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I was signed out">
    Your session expired. Sign in again. Edits you hadn't saved are lost, so save as you go.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An intake email won't send">
    The patient needs an email address on their record, and your practice's schedule time zone must be set in **Settings** → **Profile & Practice**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A patient's intake link expired">
    They can tap **Send me a new link** on the expired page. You can also send a new one from the visit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot?**, enter your email and tap **Send reset link**. Open the link from your email, set a new password, then come back to the app to sign in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
