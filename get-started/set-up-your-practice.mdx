---
title: "Set up your practice"
description: "Create the practice account and finish onboarding. The practice owner does this once."
---

Do this on a computer. It takes about 15 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the [sign-in page](https://orthoshealth.ai/sign-in) and click **Create an account**. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and your **Practice name**. Click **Create account**.

    If you're asked to confirm your email, do that, then sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Onboarding has eight short steps: Profile, Practice, Discovery, Team, Preferences, Brand, Launch and Tour. Click **Continue** to move on, or **Back** to change an answer.

    Have these ready: your practice address, who's leading the rollout, where your schedule comes from today, and how notes get into your EHR.

    Check **Make my notes sound like me** if you want notes written in your style. You'll finish that in AI Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your workspace">
    Click **Start workspace**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Names you add on the Team step aren't invited yet. Send invitations from **Settings** → **Users**. See [invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team).
</Note>

## Before your first visit

- **Set your schedule time zone.** In **Settings** → **Profile & Practice**, click **Save schedule timezone**. Intake emails and the EHR extension need it.
- **Invite your team.** See [invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team).
- **Add your visit types.** An Administrator does this in **Settings** → **Visit types & report templates**.
- **Review your intake questions** in **Settings** → **Intake Forms**. See [patient intake](/visits/patient-intake).
