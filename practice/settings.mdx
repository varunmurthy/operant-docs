Open **Settings** on the web.

## Set these first

1. **Profile & Practice**: your practice details, logo, default provider and **schedule time zone**. Each section has its own save button. Intake emails and the EHR extension need the time zone.
2. **Users**: invite your team. See [invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team).
3. **Visit types & report templates**: an Administrator sets these up. See [templates and macros](/practice/templates-and-macros).

## Everything else

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **My report templates** | Each provider picks the templates for their own visits. |
| **Intake Forms** | The questions patients answer before their visit. |
| **Patient Guide** | Your practice's design for post-visit guides. |
| **Document Library** | Your forms, and the designer to build new ones. In beta. |
| **AI Settings** | How notes are written, including your personal writing style. |
| **Insurance & Billing** | Eligibility checks, if your practice uses them. See [eligibility checks](#eligibility-checks). |
| **Review Collection** | Ask patients for reviews and collect testimonials. |

In the app, **More** → **Settings** shows your account, practice and role, and your visit types.

## Eligibility checks

If your practice uses eligibility checks, set them up in **Settings** → **Insurance & Billing**:

1. Turn on **Enable eligibility checks**.
2. Add your **Provider organization** and **Provider NPI**.
3. Leave **Run automatically when card details are ready** on to check coverage without waiting for staff.
4. Choose how long to **Reuse recent results for**, then save.

With automatic checks on, Operant Health runs a check as soon as a card scan clearly reads a supported payer and member ID. The result shows **Automatic** in the check history.

### Card scans

Upload card scans from the patient's **Insurance & eligibility** tab on the web. One image or PDF can hold both sides of the card. JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF all work.

A scan only fills in fields that are empty. If it reads something different from the saved record, the saved values are kept and the card shows **The scan differs from the saved record**. Open the card to compare.

### Run or re-run a check

On the **Insurance & eligibility** tab, pick the **Date of service** and **Service type**, then click **Run check** or **Re-run**. The service type starts on **Specialist office visit**. Benefits for the service you checked are listed first.

Click the star on a benefit to pin it. Stars set before this update were reset, so star your key benefits again.

### Missing information

If a patient's ID or insurance is incomplete, the tab lists it under **Missing information**. Click **Add missing items to Tasks** to queue a follow-up for your front desk. See [tasks](/practice/tasks).
